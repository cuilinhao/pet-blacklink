# From pet photos to a digital portrait: a Pettenderly guide

Pettenderly is a web-based AI pet portrait studio for cat and dog owners. This guide explains the reference-photo workflow and the limits to consider before purchasing credits. The examples on the website are illustrative AI images, rather than promises of an identical result.

## 1. Choose your reference photos

Use one to five clear photos of the same pet. Choose photos that show the eyes, face and coat markings, preferably in natural light. Only upload photos you have permission to use. Keeping the reference set focused on one cat or dog makes the intended subject clear.

## 2. Pick a portrait style

The main portrait studio offers styles including Watercolor Portrait, Royal Nobility Portrait and Window Seat. The styles work with cats and dogs. Choose the look that suits the keepsake you want, then upload the reference photos and generate a portrait.

## 3. Review before downloading

Generated faces, markings and fine details can vary. Inspect the eyes, ears, fur patterns and background before keeping an image. Similarity is not guaranteed. The output is a downloadable digital image; this is not a physical printing or delivery service.

## Credits and plans

The one-time Starter Pack costs US$4.99 and includes 12 credits that do not expire. One successful image uses one credit; failed generations do not consume credits. Pro costs US$9.90 per month or US$79 per year, with 40 credits supplied each month on either plan. Unused monthly credits do not roll over. Check the current website for plan details before buying. There is no free-generation allowance promised in this guide.

## Beta tools

You & your pet brings separate person and pet reference photos together into a portrait. Photo cleanup supports describing changes such as removing a distracting background cable. Both are Beta features, and faces and fine details still need review.
