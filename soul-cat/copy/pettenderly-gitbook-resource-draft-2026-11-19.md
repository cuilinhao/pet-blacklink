# Pettenderly resource guide

This is brand-authored documentation for the Pettenderly AI pet portrait studio. It covers choosing reference photos, understanding results, and deciding which credit plan fits your use. It is not an independent review or an endorsement by GitBook.

## What can I create?

Pettenderly turns reference photos of cats and dogs into downloadable AI portraits. Available looks include watercolor, royal portraits and travel-inspired scenes. The main pet portrait styles work with both cats and dogs. The result is a digital image, not a physical print.

## What should I upload?

Upload one to five photos of the same pet. Use clear reference photos with visible eyes and coat markings, preferably in natural light. Only use photos you have permission to upload. Keeping the reference set focused on the same pet helps communicate the intended subject.

## How do I start?

Open the official [Pettenderly studio](https://pettenderly.com/), choose a style, upload the pet photos, then generate a portrait. Keep the studio page open while the image is being created. Review the result before downloading a favorite image.

## Will it preserve every detail?

No exact similarity is guaranteed. Faces, markings and small details can vary in AI-generated images. Look closely at the eyes, ears, coat patterns and background before keeping a result. Illustrative style examples on the website do not guarantee an identical customer result.

## How are credits charged?

A successful image uses one credit. Failed generations do not use a credit. The one-time Starter Pack costs US$4.99 for 12 non-expiring credits. Pro costs US$9.90 per month or US$79 per year, and supplies 40 credits each month on either plan. Unused monthly credits do not roll over. This guide does not promise any free-generation allowance. See the [current plans](https://pettenderly.com/pricing) before buying.

## What do the Beta tools do?

You & your pet combines separate reference photos of a person and a pet into a portrait together. Photo cleanup supports describing edits, such as removing a distracting cable from a background. Both are Beta features, and faces and fine details still need review. The main pet portrait studio remains the place to begin.

## Where can I find help?

Contact the public support address support@soulpaw.art for product questions. The official website contains the current product, pricing and policies. Pettenderly and SoulPaw are operated by the same team, so SoulPaw may appear at checkout or in support correspondence.
