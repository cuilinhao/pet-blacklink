# Pettenderly photo-preparation checklist

DRAFT — not submitted or published on Naya. A substantive guide is prepared for possible brand-resource publishing only after the account and current publishing rules are verified. Do not treat this file as a platform receipt.

## Before choosing a photo

Pettenderly is an online AI pet portrait studio for cat and dog owners. It uses reference photos to create a downloadable digital portrait. Before uploading, check that you have permission to use each photo. Use reference images of one pet rather than mixing different cats or dogs in the same set.

Choose one to five clear photos. Look for visible eyes, recognizable coat markings and natural light. A clear view of the pet’s face helps communicate which features matter. You do not need to promise a professional photo shoot, a particular camera or a guaranteed output: the website’s workflow begins with your own pet photos.

## Pick the style you want

The main portrait studio offers looks including Watercolor Portrait, Royal Nobility Portrait and Window Seat. These portrait styles work with both cats and dogs. Choose the style, upload the reference photos, generate a portrait and review the result before downloading a favorite.

## Review the result carefully

AI-generated images can change faces, markings and fine details. Check the eyes, ears, fur patterns and background. An exact likeness is not guaranteed, and illustrative examples are not promises of identical results. The finished output is a digital image that you can download; Pettenderly is not described here as a physical-printing or shipping service.

## Understand the credit model

One successful image uses one credit. Failed generations do not consume credits. The Starter Pack costs US$4.99 once and supplies 12 non-expiring credits. Pro costs US$9.90 monthly or US$79 annually, with 40 credits supplied each month on either plan. Unused monthly credits do not roll over. No free-generation allowance is promised in this guide. Check the current official pricing before making a purchase.

## Know which features are Beta

You & your pet combines separate person and pet reference photos into a portrait together. Photo cleanup supports describing edits such as removing a distracting background cable. Both are Beta features, with faces and fine details still needing review. Begin with the main pet portrait studio when your goal is a styled portrait of a cat or dog.
